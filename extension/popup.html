Optimizely incident, November 16, 2026

Chrome grants access to saved browsing history. This check only searches from the first reported observation through the end of that UTC day. It displays matching hostnames and times on this device. History is never sent or stored by the extension.

    A match indicates a saved visit to a listed hostname. It does not establish whether malicious code ran. A missing match does not establish safety.
